⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation, Lucia’s on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, is reported to disappear once that chapter ends, and the game has no mission replay. Do it before you leave Chapter 1, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. (GTABase & PowerPyx, via EGamers; OutputLag.)

Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies (PowerPyx)

    Cheats switch trophies and saving off (GTABase, TechWiser), so leave them off for the whole run. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus — manual saves at the start of every chapter are the safety net (OutputLag).
